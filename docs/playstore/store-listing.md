## Full description

```
Vector Vanguard is a fast, retro-styled arcade space shooter built for quick
sessions and high-score chasing. Pilot your ship, dodge incoming enemies, and
blast your way up the ranks — from CADET all the way to LEGEND OF THE FLEET.

FEATURES
• Tight, responsive touch controls — drag to move, tap or hold to fire
• Three difficulty levels (Easy / Normal / Hard) with a rising challenge curve
• Power-ups: shields, rapid fire, spread shot, and extra lives
• Combo scoring — chain kills quickly to multiply your score
• Sentinel mini-boss battles every few hundred points
• A full military-style rank ladder that tracks your progress
• Synthesized retro sound and music — no audio files, tiny download size
• Local high scores and a custom callsign — no account or sign-up needed
• Fully offline — no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection

Simple to pick up, hard to put down. How high can you climb?

Developed by Code Pariwar Pvt. Ltd.
```
