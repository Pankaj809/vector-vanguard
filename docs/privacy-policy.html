Privacy Policy — Vector Vanguard

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Code Pariwar Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us") operates the mobile game Vector Vanguard (package name com.codepariwar.vector_vanguard). This page explains what data the app does — and does not — handle.

Data collection

Vector Vanguard does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not require an account, sign-up, or login. It does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and it makes no network requests during gameplay.

Local device storage

The app stores a small amount of information directly on your device only, using standard browser local storage inside the app:

  • Your high score
  • Your selected difficulty and audio/autofire preferences
  • An optional callsign (nickname) you choose to enter

This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or to any third party, and is removed automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

The Android app declares the standard internet permission used internally by its app framework to load the game's built-in interface. It is not used to send or receive personal data.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal data from any user, this applies equally to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Advertising & in-app purchases

Vector Vanguard currently contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first and the change will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
